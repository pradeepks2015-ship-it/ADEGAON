Privacy Policy — Vasuli Tracker (Adegaon)

About this app: This is not an official website of the department. This app is made for the internal office use of Adegaon DC, Lakhnadon Division, Seoni Circle. It is used only by the field staff (linemen and the Junior Engineer) to record and track electricity bill recovery. It is not a public service, it does not collect any payment, bank, UPI or card details, and it does not imitate any other website or app.

What this app is

Vasuli Tracker is used by the department's own staff (linemen and the Junior Engineer) to keep a record of electricity bill recovery for the consumers of their headquarters — which consumers have dues and which have paid — and to prepare reports from it.

What information is recorded

The app keeps the following information, which is entered by the staff (consumers do not use the app themselves):

  • Consumer's name and father's/husband's name
  • Electricity connection / consumer number (account no.)
  • Mobile number and address
  • Tariff / category and the sanctioned load of the connection (in kilowatts)
  • Current outstanding amount and payment status (paid / pending)
  • Date and amount of the last electricity bill payment
  • Which staff member entered or updated a record, and when

This data is first kept safely on the staff member's device, and is synced with our Firebase (Google Cloud) database when the network is available, so that all authorised staff get up-to-date information.

What is not collected

The app does not take GPS / location. It does not take any payment, bank, UPI or card details, and it does not process any payment itself (it only records whether a bill has been paid or not). There are no advertisements or tracking cookies, and no information is asked directly from consumers — all data is entered by the staff. The PIN / password asked in the app is only used to limit role-based access for our own staff.

Who can see the data

The data of each headquarters is visible only to the authorised staff account of that headquarters and to the Junior Engineer (JE) — this is enforced through Firebase Security Rules. The data is not sold or shared with any third party; it is used only for the department's bill-recovery work.
